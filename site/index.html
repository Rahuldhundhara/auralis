Your music.
Without the noise.

Auralis is a calm, fast desktop client for YouTube Music. Your history, playlists, radios and lyrics stay in one place — and optional cloud sync keeps them together on every device.

Everything you play

Home, Explore, Search, a 15-mood grid, YouTube Music history and private playlists — all on your own account.

≋

Player that listens

Queue-first Now Playing, synced lyrics, a 10-band EQ, sleep timer, radio and gapless playback.

⤓

Yours to keep

Offline downloads and local playlists stay on your PC. Optional Supabase sync carries settings and library across devices.

Optional cloud sync

One library, every device.

Sign in with your own Supabase Auth account in Settings → Cloud sync. Row-level security keeps your data private; your YouTube Music session and downloaded audio never leave the device.
